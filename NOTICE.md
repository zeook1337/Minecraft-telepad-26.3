# Attribution and adaptation notice

This is an independent adaptation of Telepads for Minecraft Java 26.3, begun on
2 October 2026. Telepads for Minecraft 1.19.2 is the functional and asset reference.
Its source and reused assets are credited to their original authors. The complete
GNU GPL version 3 text from that project is included as `LICENSE.md`.

Original mod: **Subaraki / ArtixAllMighty / AbsolemJackdaw**.

- Original project: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/telepad
- Original source: https://github.com/AbsolemJackdaw/Telepads2016

Credits preserved from the original mod metadata:
**Commoble** for debugging, **StrickerRocker** for bug testing,
and **Darkhax** for assistance in 2014.

Reused content includes the six item textures and reusable
translations (German, French, Russian, Chinese, Tamil and British English).
Recipes and behavior follow the original reference, with new 26.3 implementations.
The platform uses a new cuboid model and custom pixel textures generated with
OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, with the source atlas and prompt in
`docs/art/`. The original animated block renderer is not copied.
Reuse is covered by the accompanying license and
attribution.

The project logo replaces the original Telepads logo with a crop of a supplied
in-game screenshot of this adaptation. The full screenshot, 400x400 avatar and
crop provenance are in `docs/media/`. Only cropping and resizing were applied;
no AI-generated or retouched scene was used for the screenshot or logo.

Forge MDK scaffolding comes from the official **26.3-66.0.9** MDK by
Forge Development LLC and contributors. Its license notice is preserved in
`FORGE-LICENSE.txt`. The Gradle wrapper comes from that MDK unchanged.
